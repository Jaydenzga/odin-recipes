Salmon Bake is a great and easy meal for a lazy day

  • 2lbs skinless Salmon
  • paresly
  • Salt and pepper
  • 2 Cups Cooked Rice
  • Kewpie mayo
  • Seaweed paper
  • Olive oil
  1. Cook salmon gently in any preffered method, for this I usually bake it at 400 for 15 min and let it rest for 30 to finish cooking
  2. Mix the entirety of the salmon with the rice, add 1/2 cup of kewpie mayo
  3. Grab desired amount inside the seaweed paper and eat!
