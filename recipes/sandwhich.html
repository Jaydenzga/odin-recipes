Chicken Alfredo is a great and easy meal for a lazy day

  • 8oz cooked pasta
  • paresly
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4oz Cream cheese
  • Half a yellow onion
  • Garlic Cloves
  • Heavy Whipping Cream
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
  1. Before cooking, prep half a yellow onion and 4 cloves of garlic minced
  2. Saultee the onion and garlic with one TBSP of olive oil until translucent and fragrant
  3. once the previous step is finished, add 4oz of cream cheese, 4tbps butter and 1/2 cup of heavy cream to the pan until it all melds together
  4. once the sauce is melded, add parsley, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and parmesan cheese to taste
  5. When the sauce is to your liking, add in your 8oz of cooked fettucine noodles and cooked chicken
